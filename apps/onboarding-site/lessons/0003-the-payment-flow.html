Atlas Console · Lesson 3 · 1 min

Home

Home explains Atlas and points you to a useful first task.

Atlas turns a request into an integration your team can review, test, and run. AI can suggest the workflow, but it cannot approve it or access your credentials.

What you can do

  • Compose a workflow opens Create Workflow.
  • Explore capabilities shows the API actions Atlas knows about.
  • Your suggested next step changes with the selected demo role.
  • The environment card shows whether you are in Development or Production.

Add widgets if you want them

Home starts without widgets. Select Add widgets to show summaries for runs, reviews, changes, capabilities, activity, or settings. Your choices stay in this browser.
